How it works

  1. 1

    Add a deadline

    Enter the task, subject, due date and roughly how long it will take.

  2. 2

    Get a step-by-step plan

    QuickStudy splits it into research, plan, write, edit and submit, with dates.

  3. 3

    Track progress, together if you like

    Tick steps off, reschedule without stress, and share progress with a study group.

Designed to be accessible

  • Works fully with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator on every control.
  • Status is always shown with an icon and a word, never by colour alone.
  • Built to WCAG 2.0 Level AA contrast, with reduced-motion and larger-text options in Settings.
